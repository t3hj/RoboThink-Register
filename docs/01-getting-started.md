# 1. Getting started

## Signing in

1. Open the RoboThink Register address your admin gave you.
2. Enter your work email and press the sign-in button. A **magic link** is
   emailed to you. There is no password.
3. Open the **newest** email and click the link. Older links stop working.

If you see a message about your profile or role, your email has not been linked
to a staff profile yet. Ask an admin (see the [admin guide](admin-guide.md)).

## Finding your way around

| Menu item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Today at a glance, registers still to fill in, follow-ups |
| Today's Register | Marking attendance and recording lessons |
| Students | Finding, adding and editing students; each student's profile |
| Curriculum | Browsing levels and lessons |
| Reports | Lessons and progress over a date range |
| Analytics | Attendance trends and students who may need a follow-up |
| Help & guides | Short how-tos inside the app |

Admins also see **Staff**; management staff see the **Management dashboard**
(read-only).

## Your centre

If you work at more than one venue, pick it from **Working centre** in the
sidebar. The register, students and numbers shown all follow that choice. If a
student seems to be missing, check you are on the right centre first.

## RoboThink or Creative IQ

The brand switch at the top of the sidebar changes which programme's students
you see. The two are kept completely separate.
