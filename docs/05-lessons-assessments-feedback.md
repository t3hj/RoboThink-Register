# 5. Lessons, assessments and feedback

## Completed vs not finished

- **Completed** on the student's current lesson moves them on to the next.
- **Not finished** keeps them where they are. Next session, record the same
  lesson again; it is the one **Recommended** shows.
- Recording a different lesson from their current one does not move their
  progress.

If a build or other item is left aside, record what it was and why so the next
instructor sees a reminder.

## Projects

Choose **Project** instead of **Normal lesson**, pick the project, and set the
outcome. Projects are added from the student's profile and do not change their
lesson progress.

## Assessments

Assessment lessons appear in the picker like any other.

- If the student did not finish, choose **Not finished** and record the result
  when they complete it.
- When completed, record **PASS** or **FAIL**.
- A fail follows the repeat or remediation path automatically. The student then
  shows on the Dashboard under "Needs assessment / remediation follow-up" until
  it is resolved.

## Feedback sheets

Every attended session creates a feedback sheet record.

1. After you give a feedback sheet, record it from the student's row on the
   register (or use bulk **Feedback**).
2. Outstanding feedback follows the student, not the weekday, so it can be
   finished at a later catch-up.
3. The Dashboard shows how many are still outstanding.
