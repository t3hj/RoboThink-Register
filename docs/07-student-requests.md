# 7. Student requests

Only admins and managers add students. If you are an instructor and a new
student needs adding, send a request. Nothing is created until it is approved.

## Instructors: ask for students to be added

1. Open **Student requests** (or press **Request new students** on Students).
2. Choose the **Centre**.
3. For each student enter their **full name**, **subscription**, **weekly
   sessions** (day and time, with **+ Add another session** if they come more
   than once a week), and **How often**. For every-other-week students, pick a
   date in a week they attend.
4. Use **Note for the reviewer** for anything useful, such as a starting level.
5. Press **+ Add another student** to request several at once, then **Send
   requests for approval**.

Your requests are listed at the bottom of the page with their status:
**pending**, **approved**, or **declined** (with the reason).

## Admins and managers: review requests

1. Open **Student requests**. Waiting requests are at the top, and admins also
   see a banner on the Dashboard.
2. Press **Review & add**. The normal Add student form opens with the details
   filled in. Choose the starting level and lesson, check everything, then
   press **Add student**. The request is then marked approved.
3. Or press **Decline** and give a reason. The instructor sees it.

Closing the form without saving leaves the request waiting.
