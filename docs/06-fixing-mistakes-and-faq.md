# 6. Fixing mistakes and FAQ

## Fixing a mistake

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Marked the wrong student Attended or Absent | Open that day's register and press the correct button |
| Recorded the wrong lesson | Student profile, **Learning history**, edit the session |
| Session recorded twice, or on the wrong day | Student profile, **Learning history**, remove it, or edit the date |
| Forgot to record a past session | Student profile, **+ Add session** |
| Forgot to mark a whole register | Dashboard, **Registers still to fill in**, **Open register** |
| Student on the wrong lesson | Ask an admin to correct their current lesson |
| Student left | Edit them and set them **Inactive** |

Edits update the linked records together, so progress and history stay in sync.

## FAQ

**A student isn't on the register.**
Check: is the day among their **Weekly sessions**? Are they **Active**? Is the right
**Working centre** selected? Are they fortnightly and in an off week?

**Why doesn't a student's attendance % change?**
Only Attended and Absent marks count. Unmarked days and made-up absences do
not count against them.

**A student attended but I forgot the lesson.**
Mark Attended now, then use **All lessons** in the picker or fix it later from
their profile.

**Can I mark attendance for the future?**
You can open a future date, but nobody has arrived yet. Mark on the day.

**The page says something went wrong.**
Press **Try again**. If it keeps happening, open **Technical details**, screenshot
it and send it to an admin.

**A project was not finished. Has the session still been recorded?**
Yes. Saving a project session as **Not finished** records the visit and its
unfinished outcome against that project, with a linked feedback record. The
project stays active and curriculum progression does not advance. Expand the
student's **Session details** on that date to check the saved entry.

**Why has an unfinished project disappeared from Upcoming today?**
**Upcoming today** lists students still expected to arrive. An attended student
leaves that list whether their work was completed or not. A missing session or
attendance update appears separately under **Register entries to finish**.
**Not Arrived** still counts as an outstanding attendance mark.

**The session saved but another update failed.**
Use **Retry remaining updates** on that student's register entry. This finishes
the failed project-link or attendance update without recording another session.
If you have reloaded or left the page, check the saved session history first
rather than recording the same visit again.

**I can't sign in.**
Request a fresh magic link and use the newest email. If it still fails, an admin
must link your email to a staff profile.
