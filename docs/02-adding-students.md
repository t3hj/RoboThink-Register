# 2. Adding and editing students

## Who can add students

- **Admins and managers** add students directly (steps below).
- **Instructors** cannot add students themselves. They send a request that an
  admin or manager approves. See [guide 7](07-student-requests.md).

## Add a new student

1. Go to **Students** and press **+ Add student**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Full name**
   - **Date joined**
   - **Subscription** (Elite or Term Time)
   - **Centre**
   - **Weekly sessions**: a day and time for each session they attend. Press
     **+ Add another session** if they come more than once a week (for
     example Saturday and Sunday).
   - **How often**: *Every week* (default) or *Every other week*. If every
     other week, choose any date that falls in a week they attend. See
     [guide 4](04-fortnightly-and-make-ups.md).
   - **Starting level and lesson**
3. Press **Add student**.

Check the starting lesson twice. The register recommends each next lesson from
where the student started.

Only put what the app asks for. Do not add health details, photos or anything
sensitive in the notes box.

## Edit a student

Open **Students**, choose the student, then press **Edit details** on their
profile (admins and managers). You can change their sessions, how often they
attend, subscription and notes, or mark them **Inactive** when they leave.

- **Inactive** students disappear from the register but keep all their history.
- Changing their sessions only affects *future* registers.
- For a one-off different day, do not change their sessions. Use
  **+ Add to register** on that day instead.

## Things only an admin can do

Moving a student to a different centre is an admin task, and so is correcting a
student's current lesson after a transfer. Ask an admin.
