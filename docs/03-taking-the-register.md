# 3. Taking the register

## Start of session

1. Open **Today's Register**. The students expected today are grouped by
   session time.
2. The grey text at the top shows how many are recorded. Aim for everyone.
3. If someone not listed turns up, press **+ Add to register** and choose
   **Catch-up** or **Special session**.

To look at another day, use **Prev**, **Next** or the date box. Past days can be
opened and corrected.

## Students with no lesson yet

A student imported from Pebble shows **No lesson set · choose** on the register.
Open them, pick their level and lesson under **Choose starting lesson**, and
save. You can only do this once. After that their lesson moves on as you record
sessions.

## Marking attendance

- **Attended**: the student has arrived.
- **Absent**: the student is not coming.

When you press **Attended** a lesson form opens.

1. Choose **Normal lesson** or **Project**.
2. Pick the lesson actually taught. **Recommended** shows the suggested lesson;
   **Nearby** and **All lessons** let you choose another.
3. Choose **Completed** or **Not finished**.
4. Save. You can add more than one session in a day if needed.

## Marking several students at once

Tick students (or use **Select all**). A bar appears with:

- **Mark Attended** / **Mark Absent**
- **Lesson / Outcome**
- **Not Finished**
- **Feedback**

Every bulk action shows a review of who will and will not be changed before you
confirm.

## End of session checklist

- [ ] Everyone who came is **Attended**; everyone who didn't is **Absent**
- [ ] A lesson and outcome are recorded for each attended student
- [ ] Feedback sheets given today are recorded
- [ ] The "recorded" count is complete

## How attendance is counted

Only students you mark count. **Attended** counts for them, **Absent** counts
against them (unless made up, see [guide 4](04-fortnightly-and-make-ups.md)),
and a student you never marked counts as neither. That is why leaving a
register half-finished makes the percentages inaccurate. The Dashboard lists any
expected students from the last 14 days with no mark so you can go back and fix
them.
