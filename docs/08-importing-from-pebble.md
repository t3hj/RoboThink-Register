# 8. Importing students from Pebble

For admins and managers. Use this when a new venue starts, or when a term's
bookings have changed, so students don't have to be added one by one.

## Download from Pebble

Download the **activity register** for one session at one venue as a spreadsheet
(.xlsx). If a venue runs more than one weekly session, download one register per
session and import each. A student who appears in several files is matched by
name, so they are not duplicated: each file simply adds that session.

## Import

1. Open **Import from Pebble** in the menu.
2. Choose the **venue** the register is for.
3. Choose the **.xlsx file**.
4. Check the **day and start time**, which are read from the file. Each student
   gets this weekly session. Correct them if they look wrong.
5. For new students, match each **ticket** to a subscription (for example a Full
   Term Ticket is Term Time).
6. Look through the **preview**. Each student is marked:
   - **New student**: will be created
   - **Add session**: already in the app, will get this extra weekly session
   - **Already up to date**: nothing to do
   - **Listed twice** or **Needs a look**: skipped, with the reason shown
7. Press **Import**.

## What is, and isn't, brought in

Used: child name, date of birth, mobile, email and ticket type.

Ignored, and never leaves your browser: medical notes, SEN, photo permission,
year group, marketing opt-in, customer name, notes, attendance and everything
else in the file. The file itself is not uploaded anywhere.

Existing students are never overwritten. A date of birth or contact is only filled
in if the student doesn't have one yet.

## After the import

Imported students have **no lesson yet**, and they are not marked as every-other-week.

1. Assign the venue to an instructor in **Staff**.
2. The instructor opens each student, sets the **starting lesson**, and the
   Dashboard counts how many still need one. **Students**, **Needs starting
   lesson** lists them.
3. An admin or manager sets any every-other-week students by editing them.

## Looking after the data

The date of birth and contact details are personal information about children and
parents. They are visible only to admins and managers in the app, and are used for
running sessions, not marketing. Keep your privacy notice up to date, and
don't share exports.
